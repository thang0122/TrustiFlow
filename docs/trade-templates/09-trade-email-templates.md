# EMAIL TEMPLATES – INTERNATIONAL TRADE

Các mẫu dưới đây cố ý dùng placeholder. Chỉ điền dữ liệu đã xác minh. Không gửi email tự động từ TrustiFlow; người dùng kiểm tra và gửi qua hệ thống email riêng.

## 1. Reply to RFQ
**Subject:** Re: Request for Quotation [RFQ reference] – [Product]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your inquiry dated [date] regarding [product/specification]. We are reviewing the requested quantity of [quantity and unit] and the delivery terms [Incoterm and named place].

We expect to provide our quotation by [confirmed date]. To ensure the quotation is accurate, could you please confirm [missing specification / packaging / destination / payment requirement]?

Kind regards,  
[Name]  
[Title] | [Company]

## 2. Submit quotation / Pro Forma Invoice
**Subject:** Quotation [reference] for [Product]

Dear [Name],

Please find attached our quotation [reference], prepared based on the product specification and commercial terms confirmed in your request.

The offer is valid until [date and time zone]. The proposed delivery term is [Incoterm, named place, version], with payment terms [confirmed terms]. Any item not expressly listed in the quotation is excluded and has not been estimated.

Please review the attached document and let us know if any clarification is required.

Kind regards,  
[Name] | [Company]

## 6. Payment follow-up
**Subject:** Payment Follow-up – Invoice [invoice number]

Dear [Name],

Our records indicate that invoice [number], dated [date], for [amount and currency] is scheduled for payment on [contractual due date]. Could you please confirm the current payment status?

For security, please do not act on any changed beneficiary or bank details received by email alone. Any change should be verified through our previously known contact channel.

Kind regards,  
[Name] | [Company]

## 7. Bank-detail change verification
**Subject:** Independent Verification Required – Payment Details

Dear [Name],

We received a message requesting a change to the beneficiary / bank details for [transaction reference]. For security reasons, we have not updated our payment records or acted on the request.

Please confirm the request through the independent contact channel already on file, using the contact details previously verified by both parties. We will not treat a reply to the message requesting the change as sufficient verification.

Kind regards,  
[Name] | [Company]

## TrustiFlow drafting rules

- Use only known buyer/seller names, references, amounts, dates, terms and attachments.
- Use `[placeholder]` where required data is missing; do not invent.
- A generated draft is not sent. A user must review it before using it.
- For changed bank details, require an independent previously known channel; do not verify through the changed email thread.
