# INTERNATIONAL SALE OF GOODS – TERM SHEET / CONTRACT FRAMEWORK

> Use a qualified legal reviewer to turn this framework into an enforceable contract. It is not a complete legal instrument.

1. **Parties** – legal names, registration details, addresses, authorized signatories.
2. **Goods** – product name/code, specifications, quality/grade, standards, country of origin, inspection method.
3. **Quantity and tolerance** – exact quantity, unit, any agreed tolerance and measurement method.
4. **Price and currency** – item price, currency, price basis, adjustments, and what is included/excluded.
5. **Delivery** – Incoterms® rule and version, exact named place/port, delivery window, shipping mode, split shipment/transshipment if permitted.
6. **Risk and title** – separately state transfer of title; Incoterms® allocates delivery obligations and risk, but does not itself determine ownership/title.
7. **Payment** – method, due dates, deposit/milestones, bank charges, documentary credit conditions where applicable.
8. **Documents** – agreed commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, certificate of origin, insurance evidence, inspection or product-specific documents.
9. **Packaging and marking** – packaging type, labels, shipping marks, palletization and handling instructions.
10. **Inspection and acceptance** – inspection party, location, timing, criteria and procedure for claims.
11. **Warranties and claims** – scope, exclusions, notice deadlines, remedies.
12. **Compliance** – sanctions/export controls, permits, product standards, restricted goods screening where applicable.
13. **Force majeure / hardship** – events, notice, mitigation, consequences; obtain legal review.
14. **Liability and remedies** – limitations, delay, non-performance, damages; obtain legal review.
15. **Governing law / dispute resolution** – governing law, forum, arbitration/mediation terms; obtain legal review.
16. **Entire agreement / amendments / notices** – written amendment rules and valid notice addresses.
17. **Signatures** – authorized signatories, date and place.
